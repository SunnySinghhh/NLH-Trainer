Where these ranges come from

Hand-written as a consensus teaching baseline, not copied from any single published chart and not solver output. Nothing here is claimed to be optimal. Where sources disagree — and they disagree most on squeezes and multiway limped pots — a middle position was taken.

One deliberate departure: UTG+2 through the cutoff are given an open-limping range. Standard charts are raise-or-fold from every seat but the small blind, and a solver would almost never open-limp — this is a live-game line, included on request. It changes the right answer for a lot of speculative hands. The small blind completing is different: that one is standard.

Assumptions baked in: 9-max full ring, 100bb effective, a standard ~2.5bb open, and no ante or straddle. Facing much larger opens you should defend tighter than this; facing min-raises, wider. The opponent's seat is bucketed — early, middle, cutoff, button, blinds — which is how charts are taught; a solver would treat every exact pairing separately.

What is actually verified: 21 structural checks run over all 68 spots on every build. No hand is ever given two actions; ranges widen with position; defence widens against later raises; the big blind defends wider than the small blind and never folds to a limp; 4-bets are far tighter than the opens that invite them; squeezes are tighter than 3-betting the same opener heads-up; and more limpers means a tighter isolate but a wider overlimp. These catch a chart that contradicts itself — they cannot tell you it is the best strategy.
