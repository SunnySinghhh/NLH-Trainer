These are a consensus teaching baseline for 9-max cash at 100bb — close to what most full-ring charts agree on, and checked for coherence (they widen with position, and nothing opened early is folded later). They are not solver output and are not claimed to be optimal. Real play also depends on your table, your sizing, and how the players behind you respond.
